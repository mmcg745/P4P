# P4P Shared: file index (Readings, MATLAB, PCB FT, Display-day poster, Figures)

Rough search index for `P4P Shared\` under the University of Auckland OneDrive (Part IV\P4P). Paths are relative to `P4P Shared\`. Built 2026-10-13 from page 1 of each PDF, the header of each script, and the file names. Nothing in the source folder was changed.

Overview: 20 reading PDFs (mostly RWA microvibration and imbalance papers, plus a few torque-measurement papers), 16 MATLAB files (stinger design calculators, modal-plot scripts, a DAQ script, and 9 ANSYS modal exports), 7 PCB force-sensor documents, 13 poster files (current final poster, older draft and templates), and an empty `Figures\` folder.

## Readings (20 files)

- `Readings/1982_testing_and_investigation_of_RWAs.pdf`: Bosgra & Prins, 1982, "Testing and Investigations of Reaction Wheels" (ESA symposium, NLR). Measures torque and force irregularities of six European wheels near zero speed, and the effect of wheel-speed or attitude feedback on spacecraft jitter. (keywords: torque ripple, force irregularity, bearings, DC/AC motor, wheel-induced noise)
- `Readings/aas0104001-MicrovibrationAnalysisofaCantileverConfiguredReactionWheelAssembly-Published.pdf`: Zhang, Aglietti, Ren & Addari, 2014, "Microvibration analysis of a cantilever configured reaction wheel assembly" (Adv. Aircraft & Spacecraft Sci. 1(4)). Measured RWA disturbances, damping modelling, sine-sweep tests, harmonic excitation model. (keywords: cantilever RWA, damping, sine sweep, harmonic excitation, Surrey/Southampton)
- `Readings/addari-et-al-2016-dynamic-mass-of-a-reaction-wheel-including-gyroscopic-effects-an-experimental-approach.pdf`: Addari, Aglietti & Remedia, 2017, "Dynamic Mass of a Reaction Wheel Including Gyroscopic Effects: An Experimental Approach" (AIAA J. 55(1)). Experimental dynamic mass and gyroscopic effects on a cantilever RWA, with sub- and higher-harmonic amplification. (keywords: dynamic mass, gyroscopic, accelerance, harmonics, modal resonance)
- `Readings/BalancingMethod&ExperimentofRWA_DeMunter.pdf`: De Munter, Lanting, Delabie & Vandepitte (KU Leuven), year not visible, "Balancing method and experiment of a small spacecraft reaction wheel". Balancing rig for a CubeSat wheel: static unbalance cut 83%, dynamic unbalance cut 23%. (keywords: balancing, static unbalance, dynamic unbalance, correction mass, CubeSat, measurement consistency)
- `Readings/Coupled microvibration analysis of a reaction wheel assembly.pdf`: Zhang, Aglietti & Ren, 2013, "Coupled microvibration analysis of a reaction wheel assembly including gyroscopic effects in its accelerance" (J. Sound Vib. 332, 5748-5765). Coupled RWA-structure analysis with soft suspension and gyroscopic effects. (keywords: coupled analysis, accelerance, soft suspension, gyroscopic, cantilever)
- `Readings/Effect of Reaction Wheel Imbalances on Attitude and Stabilization Accuracy.pdf`: Tkachev, Mashtakov, Ivanov et al. (MDPI Aerospace), year not visible. Simulation study of how RW imbalances affect attitude pointing and stabilization accuracy. (keywords: imbalance, attitude accuracy, pointing error, stabilization, simulation)
- `Readings/Experimental and numerical investigation of coupled.pdf`: Addari, Aglietti & Remedia, 2017, "Experimental and numerical investigation of coupled microvibration dynamics for satellite reaction wheels" (J. Sound Vib. 386, 225-241). Coupled wheel-to-structure disturbance tests and models of a cantilever RWA. (keywords: coupled dynamics, dynamic mass, RWA, microvibration, experimental validation)
- `Readings/Hughes-2025-thesis .pdf`: Thomas Hughes, 2025, PhD thesis, "Investigation of soft suspension reaction wheel assembly spacecraft vibrations" (Univ. of Auckland; supervisors Aglietti and Sorokin). ## MATLAB (16 files)

- `MATLAB/stinger_design_V3.m`: Stinger design calculator, latest version (772 lines). Inputs for RWA mass, spin speed, imbalance limits and stinger geometry. Checks stiffness, buckling, stress, DAQ signal levels and imbalance (De Munter correction-mass equations). Has PASS/FAIL checks at the end. (keywords: stinger design, geometry, buckling, stress, imbalance limits, correction mass, DAQ checks)
- `MATLAB/stinger_design_V2.m`: Same calculator, earlier version v2 (580 lines). Adds the z_r sensor height, rotor radius, test-speed input and DAQ checks. Kept for history. (keywords: stinger design, v2, history)
- `MATLAB/stinger_design_V2.asv`: MATLAB autosave backup of the V2 script (24 KB). Not a real source file; safe to ignore. (keywords: autosave, backup)
- `MATLAB/stinger_design.m`: Original stinger design calculator (376 lines), v1. Fill-in inputs for frame geometry, RWA parameters, steel and aluminium material properties, and PASS/FAIL checks. (keywords: stinger design, v1, material, steel, aluminium, geometry)
- `MATLAB/modal_plots.m`: Plots modal frequency results from the parametric study. Three studies: diameter (1.6-2.2 mm at L = 50 mm), equal length changes (50, 52.5, 55, 75 mm at d = 2 mm), and axial length (50, 80, 100 mm). Plots modes 1-6 against each variable. (keywords: modal frequency, parametric study, stinger diameter, length, plots, ANSYS)
- `MATLAB/p4p_rwa_daq.m`: Acquisition script for RWA imbalance (148 lines). Six stinger channels (PCB 208C0x IEPE sensors, NI cDAQ 9234). Converts volts to newtons, applies the geometry transform to forces and moments, takes the FFT at spin frequency, and outputs static imbalance Us and heavy-spot angle. Placeholder geometry R and s. (keywords: DAQ, NI cDAQ, IEPE, PCB 208C, FFT, static imbalance, heavy spot, sensitivity calibration, Data Acquisition Toolbox)
- `MATLAB/Stinger Design parametric plots.png`: Figure from the parametric stinger study (image from file name only). (keywords: parametric plots, stinger design)
- `MATLAB/50x50x1.6mm_modes.xls`: ANSYS modal export, tab-delimited (`.xls` in name only). Mode number and frequency for a 50 x 50 x 1.6 mm stinger. (keywords: modal, ANSYS, frequency, 1.6 mm)
- `MATLAB/50x50x1.8mm_modes.xls`: ANSYS modal export, 50 x 50 x 1.8 mm. (keywords: modal, ANSYS, frequency, 1.8 mm)
- `MATLAB/50x50x2mm_modes.xls`: ANSYS modal export, 50 x 50 x 2 mm. First mode is 330.38 Hz, which matches the nominal study case. (keywords: modal, ANSYS, frequency, 2 mm, baseline)
- `MATLAB/50x50x2.2mm_modes.xls`: ANSYS modal export, 50 x 50 x 2.2 mm. (keywords: modal, ANSYS, frequency, 2.2 mm)
- `MATLAB/52.5x52.5x2mm_modes.xls`: ANSYS modal export, 52.5 x 52.5 x 2 mm. (keywords: modal, ANSYS, frequency, length study)
- `MATLAB/55x55x2mm_modes.xls`: ANSYS modal export, 55 x 55 x 2 mm. (keywords: modal, ANSYS, frequency, length study)
- `MATLAB/75x75x2mm_modes.xls`: ANSYS modal export, 75 x 75 x 2 mm. (keywords: modal, ANSYS, frequency, length study)
- `MATLAB/A80xR50x2mm_modes.xls`: ANSYS modal export, axial length 80 mm with radial length 50 mm, d = 2 mm. (keywords: modal, ANSYS, axial length, radial length, frequency)
